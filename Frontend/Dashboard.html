Frequently Asked Questions
What is a Warehouse Digital Twin? ▼
A virtual replica of your physical warehouse that uses AI/ML models to simulate operations and optimize decisions.
Which ML models are used? ▼
RandomForestRegressor for forecasting, OR-Tools for slotting, and A* algorithm for routing.
Can I upload my own dataset? ▼
Yes. Go to Upload page and drop your CSV/Excel file.
Does the system guarantee zero collisions? ▼
The A* pathfinding algorithm ensures collision-free routes for all robots.
